Turning Complex Data Into Clarity

Who I Am

I am a detail-oriented Data Analyst with a strong foundation in statistical analysis, relational databases, and executive dashboarding. I enjoy digging into ambiguous datasets, validating assumptions, and building reproducible pipelines that stakeholders can trust.

Education & Background

I hold a Bachelor’s degree in Information Systems & Analytics, where I built hands-on expertise in database design, quantitative modeling, and visual communication. Whether profiling millions of rows in SQL or automating recurring reports in Python and Excel, I focus on accuracy and business relevance.

Career Goals & Problem-Solving Approach

My goal is to grow as a Data Analyst on a product, operations, or growth team where data directly shapes strategy. I approach every problem systematically: define the business question, audit data quality, test hypotheses through Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), and present clear, actionable recommendations.
